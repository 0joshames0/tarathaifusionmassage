Fees & Travel

Transparent pricing for travel and evening appointments

Travel Fees

Travel fees are calculated from Bonney Lake, WA. The fee is based on the one-way distance to your location.

Distance from Bonney Lake Travel Fee
0 – 15 miles Free
15 – 30 miles $15
30 – 45 miles $25
Distances beyond 45 miles may be available by special arrangement. Please contact us to discuss.

Evening Surcharge

Appointments that begin in the evening carry a small surcharge to account for later hours.

Appointment Start Time Surcharge
6:00 PM – 7:00 PM +$15
7:00 PM – 8:00 PM +$20
8:00 PM – 9:00 PM +$30
The surcharge is based on the scheduled start time of the appointment.
